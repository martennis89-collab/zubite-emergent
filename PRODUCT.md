# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Zubite serves a connected dental-care ecosystem rather than a single audience. Patients use it when they need understandable guidance, help interpreting a concern, and a credible path to the right clinic. Parents acting for children are a first-class audience and will have dedicated journeys that address age-specific questions and decisions. Care Pass holders need to understand and use their benefits without confusing the pass with the core guidance experience.

Individual dentists and clinic teams use Zubite to receive and qualify enquiries, communicate with patients, manage consultations, track bookings and response times, and build a trustworthy public presence. Zubite administrators operate the marketplace, maintain quality and content, support clinics and patients, and monitor platform performance.

## Product Purpose

Zubite exists to generate qualified clinic consultations while becoming Bulgaria's trusted dental guidance platform. It should help people move from uncertainty to informed action, while giving clinics the context and tools needed to respond meaningfully.

Trust must be earned with verified clinic information, transparent matching criteria, response-time data, and expert-reviewed educational content. Success is not simply a click or a lead: it is a well-informed patient reaching a relevant clinic with clear expectations.

## Positioning

Zubite combines independent dental education, personalized orientation, and transparent clinic matching—without presenting an online diagnosis or selling any clinic as “the best.”

## Brand Personality

Zubite is calm without falling into the generic, frictionless “AI calm” aesthetic. It is trustworthy, clear, transparent, human, and premium. Its voice should reduce anxiety through direct explanations and honest expectations, while its experience remains responsive, alive, and immersive.

## Anti-references

Zubite must never appear connected to, controlled by, or biased toward a specific clinic. It should not resemble a generic AI wellness site, a sterile hospital portal, a discount marketplace, an aggressive lead-generation funnel, or a glossy luxury-clinic advertisement.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through visible independence.** Explain how guidance, matching, and recommendations work; distinguish relevance from ranking; never imply clinical certainty or hidden preference.
2. **Educate before asking.** Give users enough plain-language context to understand the problem, the available paths, and what will happen next before requesting commitment or personal information.
3. **Make every next step explicit.** Calls to action must describe the real-world outcome—visit, callback, chat, booking, or benefit use—and appear with a clear hierarchy rather than competing repetition.
4. **Feel human, alive, and composed.** Use purposeful motion, responsive feedback, layered depth, and immersive storytelling to create energy without anxiety, spectacle, or generic AI polish.
5. **Design one ecosystem across surfaces.** Patient, parent, dentist, clinic, Care Pass, and administrator experiences should share a recognizable foundation while adapting hierarchy and density to each user's job. Patterns should translate cleanly from responsive web to a future native mobile app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the required baseline. All core journeys must support keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantics, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, comfortable touch targets, and clear language. Motion must be purposeful and provide equivalent reduced-motion behavior; accessibility should preserve the product's immersive character rather than flatten it. Designs must work for older users, parents under decision pressure, and people with varied levels of dental and digital literacy.
