# Zubite.bg — Product Requirements Document

## Original Problem Statement
Build and optimize **Zubite.bg**, an educational orthodontic platform for the Bulgarian market that helps users understand their orthodontic issues and guides them toward the correct treatment path. Built with Next.js (frontend) + FastAPI (backend) + MongoDB.

**Language**: All UI/emails/content in **Bulgarian**.

## Core Product Requirements
1. **Master Quiz** — Multi-step diagnostic with Adult/Teen/Child branching, severity scoring, lead capture, segment-specific success pages.
2. **SEO & Content** — SSR `/blog`, object-storage image uploads, sitemaps.
3. **AI Outbound Calling** — ElevenLabs Conversational AI with patient context, transcript retrieval.
4. **B2B Clinic Onboarding** — `/za-kliniki` application form, structured admin review, clinic dashboards, lead distribution.
5. **Lead Verification** — Automated 24h-after-assignment email loop asking patients to confirm clinic contact.

- **Auth Batch E5 — Session Governance (May 2026)** — Upgraded admin/clinic auth from token-only to server-side session governance backed by a new `auth_sessions` Mongo collection. **JWT payload** now includes a `jti` (UUID) on every issued token; `iat` + `role` are also explicitly set. **Login** (`POST /api/admin/login`, `POST /api/clinic/login`) creates a matching `auth_sessions` row with `{user_id, user_type, jti, created_at, last_seen_at, expires_at, revoked_at, revoked_reason}` — no raw IP/UA stored. **Protected reads** (`get_current_user`, `get_current_clinic`) decode the JWT, then look up the session by `jti` and reject when missing, revoked, expired, wrong `user_type`, or mismatching `user_id` (returns 401 / 403 consistently). `last_seen_at` refreshed at most once per 60s. **Logout** (`POST /api/admin/logout`, `POST /api/clinic/logout`) revokes the current jti in addition to clearing the cookie — best-effort jti extraction so logout never errors on stale tokens. **New endpoints**: `POST /api/admin/logout-all` and `POST /api/clinic/logout-all` revoke every active session for the current user; `POST /api/admin/auth-sessions/cleanup-expired` (admin-only) flips expired sessions to revoked for housekeeping. Admin-token cannot reach clinic routes and vice-versa (cross-role rejection at session level, not just JWT-role). **Backwards compatibility note**: legacy pre-E5 tokens (no jti) are now rejected — users must re-login once. Public routes (review submission, recommended-clinics, public clinic profile, blog) remain fully public.
